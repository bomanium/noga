# Odd and broken files

Asked 2026-11-04, at the start of the backlog's "Robustness on odd files" (decisions/2026-11-03-launch-readiness.md).
Before asking, 48 odd and broken files made with ffmpeg were checked: truncated (cut at 1000 bytes, or at half with
the moov first or last), overwritten in the middle, empty, random bytes, audio-only and video-only, variable frame
rate, an edit list, one frame, drop-frame and 23.976 timecode, several video and audio streams, 5.1 and eight mono
tracks, odd and tiny frame sizes, UHD 10-bit 4:2:2, H.264 4:2:2 10-bit and 4:4:4, ProRes 4444, HDR (PQ HEVC, HLG
ProRes), rotation, MPEG-2, MJPEG, MKV.

What they showed:

- **Crashes**: H.264 in 4:2:2 10-bit or 4:4:4 (MP4 or MOV) went to the managed path, whose ShotDetector takes decoded
  frames only in 4:2:0, 4:2:2 8-bit and 4:2:0 10-bit: an unhandled ArgumentException (exit 134). And in the web UI and
  the app, any exception but a read error ended the queue's worker: every file after it stayed queued.
- **Read errors that had frames to report**: an MP4 cut short with its moov first, or overwritten in the middle,
  stopped the managed path ("Sample 108 lies outside the file", "NAL unit length overruns the packet"), where the
  FFmpeg path reads what there is (as it did for the same MOVs).
- **No finding for what was lost**: a ProRes MOV cut in half decoded 38 of its 75 frames, one overwritten lost frames,
  and the report said nothing of either.
- Audio-only files were a read error ("has no video stream"); the rest read and were reported.

## Fixed without asking

- `ManagedMeasurer.CanMeasure` sends H.264 in other pixel formats to the FFmpeg path.
- `Checker.Run` gives a file the managed path starts on but can't read to the end to the FFmpeg path, and turns any
  other fault into that file's error ("can't check FILE: ... (a fault in Noga: please report it)", exit 2); `Jobs`
  fails the one job on anything a check throws and goes on.
- `OddFileTests` checks 26 of the files: a report, or a read error that names the file; never a fault.

## A file whose picture or sound ends early, or that has frames that can't be decoded

- **A failure** (recommended): a `decode` check, a failure when the decoded picture or sound ends more than one frame
  before the container says, or when frames can't be decoded; it says how much was read and where it stopped, checked
  against ffmpeg -v error -i FILE -f null - (its errors, and the last frame's time).
- A warning: the same, a warning (some containers' durations are approximate).
- Reported only.

**Answer: a failure.**

## Audio-only files

- **Check their audio** (recommended): loudness, true peak, tone, silence, channels and format on audio-only files (WAV
  or MOV audio stems, AAC in M4A), the picture's checks reported as not measured. Audio stems are a common delivery
  item.
- Keep the read error: Noga is for video.

**Answer: check their audio.**
