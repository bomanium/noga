# A long check's progress

Asked 2026-11-04, the third of the second UI/UX review's top five (decisions/2026-11-04-rows-in-place.md): a check
showed only "measuring 4 min 10 s" while it ran, so a 60-minute ProRes master gave no idea for about 17 minutes how
far it had got.

## How progress shows in the web UI's and the app's rows

- **Percent and time left** (recommended): "measuring 42% · about 9 min left", from how far into the file the
  decoding is; both decoding paths report their position
- Percent only: "measuring 42%", no estimate that can be wrong early on
- Skip this item

**Answer: percent and time left.**

## What was built

- `CheckProgress` (Noga.Ffmpeg): a check's stages, each weighted by its share of the time (docs/notes/performance.md):
  on the FFmpeg path the picture pass 78% (each frame's time against the file's duration), the shots' second decode
  20% (ShotDetector's own progress), the sound 2% (each audio track done); on the managed path the one pass that
  measures and detects the shots 97% (ShotDetector's progress over its frames), the sound 3%. It reports every half
  percent or more. `FfmpegMeasurer.Measure`, `ManagedMeasurer.Measure` and `Checker.Run` take an
  `IProgress<double>`.
- `Jobs` keeps it on the job (`Job.Progress`, each whole percent, so the events aren't sent more than a hundred times
  a check); `Job.Time` says "measuring 42% · about 9 min left" (the time so far times what is left over what is done,
  shown once 2% in and 5 s along), "measuring 12 s" before the first report, "took 3 min 05 s" once done. The web UI
  writes the same from the job's `progress`; neither rebuilds the row for a new percent, only its time line.
- On a 100 s ProRes programme (make-samples.sh's, looped) in the web UI: 3% at once, then up about 3% every 2 s,
  "about 2 min left" at 6% falling to "under a minute left" at 19%, done in 57 s.
